How Blip rental works

  • Pay-per-play, not a lease. Traditional rental is usually a fixed multi-week contract on one face. On Blip you choose boards, set any daily budget, and pay for the plays you buy — pause or stop anytime. No long-term lease and no minimum spend.
  • Digital rotation. Each board runs a loop of short spots (typically about 7.5–10 seconds). Your ad shares the loop with other advertisers instead of owning the face full-time.
  • Availability can change. These counts are from our last update, not a reservation. The marketplace shows what’s open when you start a campaign.

Houston billboard advertising FAQs

›Do I need a contract or minimum spend to advertise in Houston?

No. Blip is self-serve with no minimum spend and no long-term contract. You set a daily budget, and your ad rotates on the boards you choose. You can pause or stop your campaign at any time.

›Can I pick exactly which Houston boards my ad runs on?

Yes. You choose specific boards when you build your campaign — the Houston inventory map and board list on this page show every active Blip board labeled in Houston, including its street address, size, and estimated daily impressions among adults 18 and over, adjusted for share of voice.

›Why do two billboards on the same road cost different amounts?

Blip charges per play, and every face has its own audience and its own demand. Two boards that look similar on the map can differ by facing, read, traffic, and advertiser competition. Confirm live play prices when you build a campaign — see the Texas page for statewide measured dispersion examples.
